Spider-Man Movie Webpage

Context

I created a webpage about a Spider-Man movie to practice my HTML and CSS skills. I wanted to share information about Spider-Man, the characters, and the movie’s story.

Content

My webpage introduces Spider-Man, a superhero who uses his powers to protect people and keep his city safe. It highlights the movie’s story and characters and explains what makes Spider-Man an exciting hero.

Reflection

One challenge was making the webpage look organized and visually appealing while using HTML and CSS. I learned that I can improve a page by carefully adjusting its styles and checking how each change affects the layout.

My biggest takeaway was that building a webpage takes planning and patience. If I had more time, I would add more details about the characters and improve the design.
