# Part A: Existing Technology

- **FIFA+** — Watches soccer matches, highlights, and other soccer videos.
- **OneFootball** — Follows teams, scores, news, and match updates.
- **FotMob** — Shows live scores, match stats, and soccer news.
- **SofaScore** — Tracks live scores, player ratings, and match statistics.
- **ESPN** — Provides soccer scores, news, analysis, and some live coverage.
- **Google Sports** — Shows team schedules, scores, and match results.
- **Flashscore** — Gives live scores and results for soccer leagues.
- **theScore** — Provides soccer scores, news, and notifications.
- **365Scores** — Tracks teams and sends live match updates.
- **LiveScore** — Shows live scores and fixtures.
- **Transfermarkt** — Shares information about players, clubs, and transfers.
- **BeSoccer** — Provides soccer news, scores, and team information.
- **Soccerway** — Lists fixtures, results, standings, and team details.
- ** FotMob** — Offers match commentary and detailed statistics.
- **YouTube** — Hosts soccer highlights, tutorials, and team videos.
- **Twitch** — Streams soccer-related discussions and some live sports coverage.
